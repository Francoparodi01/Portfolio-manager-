# Compare Against HOLD

## Workflow

1. Use the Decision Lab view selected by the task.
2. Preserve horizon, costs, PIT quality and sample size.
3. Keep PLAN, HOLD and alternative counterfactuals on the same basis.
4. Separate observed DVA/EV from realized account PnL.
5. Report insufficiency when the cohort or replay quality cannot support the comparison.

## Verification

Never infer causal superiority from a raw average. Do not mix horizons or add 5D/10D/20D outcomes together.
